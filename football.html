how it works

Paste the links you already watch — a YouTube game, a live channel, a legal stream from a service you pay for. Each link opens in a tile. Use 2-up for a main game plus RedZone, or 4-up for the whole slate.

Click the audio button on a tile to move sound there without reloading the video. Use the arrows or 1–4 to move around, and open TV mode when you are on the couch.

what it is not

multiview does not provide games, streams or a subscription. It is a player for links you have the right to watch. If a site refuses to be framed, it opens in its own window.
