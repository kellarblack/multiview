the bridge

Run Threadfin or xTeVe in front of your tuner or Plex live TV, then export the M3U and XMLTV. Add the M3U in Sources → IPTV. The channels appear with guide data, and you can put four on one screen.

Local Plex and tuner addresses are plain HTTP. Use the downloaded build on localhost, or put the stream behind HTTPS.

library vs live

This is for live channels and direct stream URLs. Plex library movies and shows are not exposed as embeddable links; use Plex for those.

No Plex login is stored. The guide and playlist stay in this browser.
