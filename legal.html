What this site is

multiview.cc is a viewer. You supply links; the page displays them in frames or windows. It does not host, store, upload or transmit the video itself, and it does not bypass logins, paywalls or regional restrictions. Every third-party site you open keeps its own terms and privacy policy.

Privacy

  • No account is required. This build has no sign-in and no server storing your boards.
  • Boards and settings stay in your browser. They are saved in localStorage for this address. Clearing site data removes them.
  • No analytics or ad trackers ship with the page.
  • Streaming sites see what they normally see — your IP and browser, subject to their policies and any referrer rules.
  • If cloud accounts are switched on later, synced boards will be stored server-side and that change will be labelled before you sign in.

A downloaded beta build keeps working as it is. The static files are not DRM and can be copied, so pro access applies to the hosted service, not to a lock on your copy.
