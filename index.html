embeds

what plays inside a tile, and what needs its own window.

Embedding is decided by the site's own X-Frame-Options / CSP header — no multiview app can override it. Any other link from an index works the same way: paste it, and if it refuses framing, pop it out. Use your own accounts and logins; nothing here bypasses a paywall. what can I paste?

Any web link. YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Rumble, Vimeo and Dailymotion links are converted to their player versions automatically; everything else is loaded as a page. Several links at once work too — separate them with spaces, commas or new lines, and each becomes a tile.

where is this tile from?

Right-click a tile, press I, or use the info button in its hover pill. The panel shows the type, host, source and player URLs, framing status and audio. The Headers tab shows what the browser allows (cross-origin frames hide their response headers); View switches tile headers on or off — they are off by default.

why is a tile black, or showing an error?

The site refuses to be embedded — it sends X-Frame-Options or a CSP rule that blocks framing, and no web page can override that. Some sites go further and show only their chat or a blank box when framed. Use More → Window wall (W) to open every tile as a real window. A few known sites (StreamCorner, DamiTV, CXtv, IPTV Web, TVNow and the other aggregators) skip the tile entirely and open straight in a window; any other site that redirects out of a tile is detected the first time and remembered.

Direct stream files (.m3u8, .mp4) play in the built-in player instead of a frame, but the server has to allow cross-origin reads — if it doesn't, the tile says so.

what about ads and pop-ups?

Every tile is sandboxed, so a site inside it cannot open pop-up windows, redirect this page, trigger a download or spam alert boxes. Ads painted inside a stream come from that site and can't be removed from outside the frame — a browser ad blocker (uBlock Origin) is the only way to kill those, and it covers pop-out windows too.

how do I move a tile?

Hover a tile and drag it by the grip in its top-right corner, or press V (or the four-way arrow in the toolbar) to arrange, then hold anywhere on a tile and drag it. Drop it on another tile to swap the two.

why is everything muted?

Browsers block autoplay with sound, and four streams talking at once is chaos. Unmute the one you're watching with the speaker button in its header. That only works on YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Rumble, Vimeo and Dailymotion — on other sites use the mute control inside their own player.

how do I listen to just one game?

Hover a tile and press the headphones button. That tile becomes the audio source and every other tile whose player accepts a mute setting is muted. The selected tile gets an AUDIO chip so it is clear which one was chosen.

On sites the app cannot control, muting has to happen inside their own player — the toast says so instead of pretending it worked.

can I get captions?

On YouTube tiles the CC button in the tile controls turns YouTube's own captions on or off — most live news and major sports streams ship them. Turn it on once and new YouTube tiles start with captions already on. For Twitch and everything else, use the captions button inside that player; the streamer has to provide them.

Sites that supply no captions at all can't be captioned from inside the page — a web page has no access to another site's audio. Use what's built into your machine instead: Chrome Live Caption (Settings → Accessibility → Live Caption) or macOS Live Captions (System Settings → Accessibility → Live Captions). Both caption any audio playing in the browser, iframe included, with nothing to set up here.

why did a stream stop working after a while?

Live aggregators rotate domains and rate-limit, so a page can start answering 403 or simply go blank. reload the tile, or grab the site's current address from the embeds panel.

where are my boards saved?

In this browser's localStorage, per address. Same browser, same address — same boards. A private window, a different port or a cleared cache starts empty. Nothing is uploaded.

can I download it for my computer?

Yes. Use Download for computer on Home or in Plan, unzip the folder and run the start script. It runs locally, keeps working offline, and there is no install prompt or home-screen app.

why are the YouTube controls missing?

YouTube tiles run in a clean mode: the site's own pause/fullscreen bar is hidden and the frame ignores the pointer, so hovering never brings it back. Use the tile's hover pill for mute, audio focus, captions, reload, focus and fullscreen — or pop the tile out if you want YouTube's player. Live streams don't need seeking; this is deliberate.

does it work on Apple TV or a smart TV?

Apple TV has no web browser. AirPlay or Screen Mirroring from a Mac or iPhone shows the wall on the TV. Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV browsers can open multiview.cc/tv directly — arrow keys move, Enter focuses a tile.

will four screens be slow?

Four tiles are four video players. 1–2-up is the lightest; 4-up is still fine on a normal laptop, phone or TV. Give the last tile a second to start.

can I add more than 4 tiles?

Four per board is the cap. Layouts are screen counts: picking 2-up shows the first two tiles. Make a second board with Shift+N — tabs keep their own layout and links, so "NFL" and "F1" never interfere.

can I watch on two monitors?

Yes. Use More → Window wall (W) and drag the windows wherever you like; each one keeps its own URL. Or pop out individual tiles and leave the board on the second screen.

does this get around a login or paywall?

No. Tiles are just frames. If a site needs an account, you sign in inside that tile with your own account, and paid content still needs your subscription. We never see or store your password.

Paid services — Hulu, Disney+, Netflix, Prime Video, Max, Fox, Peacock, Paramount+ and Apple TV — use DRM and refuse to be framed. Paste one and it opens in its own window, where you sign in normally; it cannot be tiled. YouTube videos and live channels embed; YouTube TV does not.
