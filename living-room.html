tv mode

Open multiview.cc/tv. Arrows move, Enter focuses, 1–4 jump, Y exits. The top bar gets out of the way and the grid fills the screen.

fullscreen web app

The site ships a fullscreen web app manifest. On Android TV and Google TV browsers that support install, add it to the home screen. On a computer, use More → fullscreen.

Apple TV

Apple TV has no web browser. AirPlay from an iPhone, iPad or Mac is the current path. A native tvOS app is a separate project, not something a static site can fake.
